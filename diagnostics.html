Mission

Provide engineering-level marine electrical diagnostics that identify the root cause of equipment failures through measurement, analysis, and documented findings.

Every investigation is conducted using a structured troubleshooting process that emphasizes objective data over assumption. Diagnostic conclusions are supported by electrical measurements, thermal analysis, network evaluation, and system performance under actual operating conditions.

The objective is not simply to restore operation, but to determine why the failure occurred so corrective action addresses the underlying deficiency rather than the symptom.

Diagnostic Process

Marine electrical systems have become increasingly integrated, combining high-current power distribution, digital communications, battery management, networked electronics, and automated control systems. As a result, many faults cannot be isolated through visual inspection or conventional troubleshooting alone.

Each investigation follows a structured engineering process intended to eliminate possible failure modes until the source of the fault has been verified.

1. Define the Fault

Review system configuration, reported symptoms, environmental conditions, equipment history, and previous repair attempts to establish the scope of the investigation.

2. Acquire Measurements

Collect electrical, thermal, mechanical, RF, and network data while equipment is operating under actual load conditions. Measurements establish objective evidence of system performance.

3. Isolate Root Cause

Systematically eliminate potential failure modes through targeted testing until the source of the malfunction has been verified. Corrective actions are based upon measured evidence rather than component substitution.

4. Recommend Corrective Action

Provide findings, supporting measurements, and repair recommendations that address the underlying cause of failure while reducing the likelihood of recurring faults.

Engineering Capabilities

Sure Shot Marine provides engineering-level diagnostic services for marine electrical and electronic systems. Diagnostic work is organized by system capability rather than by individual instruments. Measurement methods are selected according to the reported fault, operating conditions, and system architecture to verify equipment performance and isolate root cause.

Diagnostic Findings

When requested, diagnostic findings can be documented to support repair planning, insurance claims, vessel surveys, maintenance records, or future service work. Documentation may include measured values, thermal images, waveform captures, photographs, and observations collected during the investigation.

Recommendations are based upon measured evidence and are intended to provide a clear basis for corrective action. Where additional testing is warranted, the scope and rationale are identified before repairs proceed.
