About Sure Shot Marine

Direct service from an experienced owner-led team.

Jeremyn Horsley

Owner & Lead Marine Electrician

Sure Shot Marine is an owner-led marine electrical business serving boat owners throughout the San Francisco Bay Area.

The company is led by Jeremyn Horsley, an ABYC Certified Marine Electrician and electrical engineer. His background includes electrical engineering, industrial power and commissioning, Navy aviation electronics, and Coast Guard shipboard navigation, communications, and electronic systems.

That experience shapes a careful, methodical approach to electrical troubleshooting, repair, and onboard system improvements.

A Team Built on Trust

A Practical, Methodical Approach

The problem is not always where it appears. Sure Shot Marine tests the system on the boat, isolates the fault, and explains the findings before moving ahead with repairs.

Whether the job involves an intermittent fault, a charging problem, or an onboard system improvement, the focus remains the same: find the cause, explain the options clearly, and deliver dependable work.
