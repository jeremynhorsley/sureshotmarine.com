About

Sure Shot Marine LLC

Jeremyn Horsley

  • ABYC Certified Marine Electrician.
  • Electrical Engineer

Sure Shot Marine provides professional marine electrical troubleshooting, repair, and system support throughout the San Francisco Bay Area.

Founded by Jeremyn Horsley, an ABYC Certified Marine Electrician and electrical engineer, Sure Shot Marine takes a practical, methodical approach to marine electrical work—from tracking down difficult electrical problems to upgrading and improving onboard systems.

Jeremyn's background includes electrical engineering, industrial power and commissioning, along with service in the U.S. Navy working on aviation electronics and in the U.S. Coast Guard maintaining shipboard navigation, communications, and electronic systems. That experience brings an emphasis on careful troubleshooting, attention to detail, and doing the job right.

Credentials

  • B.S. Electrical Engineering, San Francisco State University, 2016
  • U.S. Coast Guard — Electronics Technician, 2007-2018
