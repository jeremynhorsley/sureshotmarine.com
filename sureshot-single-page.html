Marine Electrical Services

AC and DC electrical troubleshooting, repair, system upgrades, and commissioning.

Battery & Charging Systems

Battery banks, alternators, regulators, shore chargers, DC-DC chargers, charging faults, and performance testing.

AC & Shore Power

Shore power, inlets, breakers, grounding, GFCI protection, transfer equipment, inverter/chargers, and AC distribution.

DC Electrical Systems

Distribution panels, busbars, circuit protection, switching, wiring faults, voltage drop, and critical circuits.

Lithium Systems

LiFePO4 conversions, BMS integration, charging compatibility, alternator protection, and commissioning.

Navigation & Communications

NMEA 2000, VHF, AIS, GPS, antenna systems, network faults, and equipment integration.

Corrosion & Stray Current

Bonding systems, galvanic protection, hull-potential testing, stray-current investigation, and abnormal anode loss.

Electrical Diagnostics

Electrical faults are tested under operating conditions to identify the source of the problem before equipment is repaired or replaced.

Electrical Testing

AC and DC voltage, current, voltage drop, contact resistance, conductor resistance, leakage current, and parasitic loads.

Thermal Imaging

Thermal evaluation of connections, breakers, conductors, charging equipment, shore-power components, and distribution systems.

Battery & Power Analysis

Battery and charging-system testing, AC power quality, generator output, inverter performance, and load response.

Networks & Communications

NMEA 2000 and CAN Bus diagnostics, VHF antenna testing, feedline evaluation, SWR, and RF performance.

Corrosion Testing

Hull potential, bonding systems, galvanic conditions, stray-current investigation, and galvanic-isolator performance.

System Verification

Testing under actual operating conditions to verify system performance and confirm corrective work.

Documentation & Findings

Findings can be documented when required for repair planning, system upgrades, maintenance records, surveys, insurance-related work, or future service.

  • Measured electrical values
  • Photographs and thermal images
  • Observed deficiencies
  • Recommended corrective actions
  • System mapping and circuit documentation
